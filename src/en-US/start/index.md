Faber is a language written by models, so you do not install it by hand.
Give your model this link:

```text
https://faberlang.dev/install.md
```

Your model reads that file, downloads the current release for your machine,
verifies its checksum, and installs the `faber` command. It then writes a small
hello program and runs `faber check` on it. All of this happens on your own
machine, and your model tells you when the check passes.
